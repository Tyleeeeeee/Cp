\noindent \textbf{Solution}\\
This use a gcd properties $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(a,a-b)\text{ , }\forall a\ge b$,then 
\begin{align*}
	\gcd(a_1+b_j,a_2+b_j)&=gcd(a_1+b_j,a_2-a_1)\\
	\gcd(a_1+b_j,a_2-a_1,a_3+b_j)&=\gcd(a_1+b_j,a_2-a_1,a_3-a_1)\\
	\gcd(a_1+b_j,a_2-a_1,a_3+b_j,\dots,a_n+b_j)&=\gcd(a_1+b_j,a_2-a_1,a_3-a_1,\dots,a_n-a_1)
\end{align*}
Thus we just precompute $g=\gcd(a_2-a_1,a_3-a_1,\dots,a_n-a_1)$,then we can use $O(1)$ to construct the answer
